What this page does and doesn't know

  • Likes are real. Every number above is a genuine click, stored on a server and shared across everyone who visits.
  • Page views are not tracked yet. To see how many people visit and which pages they read, sign up free at goatcounter.com, then uncomment the analytics block at the bottom of index.html and swap in your code. Your dashboard then lives at YOURCODE.goatcounter.com.
  • The "people viewing right now" pill is simulated. It generates a plausible-looking number and is not connected to real traffic. It is not counted anywhere on this page.
  • Likes can be nudged. The counter is a public API, so someone who works out the URL could inflate a number. Fine for a class site; don't treat it as an audited vote.
